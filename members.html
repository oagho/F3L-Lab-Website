Current Lab Members

Principal Investigator

Dr. Meysam Haghshenas

Associate Professor, MIME Department

Dr. Meysam Haghshenas is an Associate Professor in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Toledo. Prior to joining UToledo in June 2019, he served as an Assistant Professor at the University of North Dakota, conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Waterloo in Canada, and worked as a Process Engineer with Linamar Corporation.

His research focuses on the mechanical behavior and processing of engineering materials, with particular emphasis on fatigue and fracture mechanics, additive manufacturing, advanced materials characterization, and processing–structure–property relationships.
