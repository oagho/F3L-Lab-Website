Welcome to F3L

Directed by Dr. Meysam Haghshenas, the Fatigue , Fracture, and Failure Laboratory (F3L) at the University of Toledo specializes in evaluation and mechanical characterization of advanced structural materials. Our lab researches lightweight alloys, high-throughput fatigue life behavior, additive manufacturing properties, and material integrity across severe environments.
