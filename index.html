Welcome to F3L

Directed by Dr. Meysam Haghshenas, the Fatigue, Fracture, and Failure Laboratory (F3L) at the University of Toledo specializes in the evaluation and mechanical characterization of advanced structural materials. Our lab investigates lightweight alloys, high-throughput fatigue behavior, additive manufacturing properties, and material integrity across severe environments.
